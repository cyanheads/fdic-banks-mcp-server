# 0.1.1 — 2026-09-26

First public release: ten tools over the FDIC BankFind API, no resources or prompts. Dollar amounts are thousands of US dollars throughout, and every data response carries `data_as_of`, the FDIC index build time.

## Added

- **`fdic_search_institutions`** — find FDIC-insured banks and savings institutions, merged and failed ones included, by name (current, former, or trade name), CERT, state, city, status, charter class, asset range, or holding-company RSSD; returns the CERT every other tool takes.
- **`fdic_get_institution_financials`** — one institution's quarterly Call Report history by CERT, most recent first, up to 200 quarters back to 1984, with its status, holding company, and successor CERT.
- **`fdic_compare_peers`** — one institution against a peer group for one quarter: peer median, quartiles, minimum, maximum, percentile, and rank per metric. Peers default to the same asset-size band nationwide; narrow them with `peer_state` or name them with `peer_certs`.
- **`fdic_query_financials`** — a multi-institution, multi-quarter panel filtered by CERTs, state, asset range, and up to five `metric_filters` thresholds, capped at `FDIC_PANEL_MAX_ROWS`; a panel larger than the inline preview is staged as a dataframe.
- **`fdic_search_failures`** — failures and assistance transactions since 1934 by name, CERT, state, date range, resolution, method, or size, with asset, deposit, and estimated-loss totals; `group_by` adds rollups per year, state, method, or insurance fund.
- **`fdic_get_deposits`** — Summary of Deposits (annual, as of June 30, 1994 on): an institution's branches and per-state market share, a state, county, city, ZIP, or MSA market ranked by deposits with its HHI, or one institution's rank and share in one market.
- **`fdic_list_reference`** — the vocabulary the other tools take, from built-in tables: metrics with FDIC field, unit, and basis, bank classes, failure methods, insurance funds, peer asset bands, and dataset coverage.
- **`fdic_dataframe_describe`, `fdic_dataframe_query`, `fdic_dataframe_drop`** — inspect, query with one read-only DuckDB `SELECT` (saved as a new table with `register_as`), and drop staged `df_<id>` tables. A tenant's dataframes share a 1,000,000-row budget, oldest evicted first.
- **Metric catalog** — 49 named Call Report metrics; unsuffixed income and return metrics are single-quarter figures, and `_ytd` names accumulate from January 1. A capital ratio or `insured_deposit_share` reported as `0` is returned as `null` (not reported).
- **Configuration** — `FDIC_RATE_LIMIT_RPS` (default 8, range 1–15), `FDIC_CACHE_TTL_SECONDS` (3600; 0 disables), `FDIC_PANEL_MAX_ROWS` (50,000; range 1,000–200,000), `FDIC_DATASET_TTL_SECONDS` (86,400; minimum 60), and `FDIC_DATAFRAME_DROP_ENABLED` (false registers `fdic_dataframe_drop` disabled). `CANVAS_PROVIDER_TYPE` defaults to `duckdb`; `none` turns staging off.
- **Request pacing** — one process-wide FDIC pacer and response cache; a saturated queue or an FDIC 429 fails as `pacer_shed` or `upstream_rate_limited` carrying `retryAfter`.
- **`.mcpb` bundle** — ships without DuckDB's native binding, so the dataframe tools there report an install hint; every other tool works.

## Dependencies

- **`@duckdb/node-api`** ^1.5.5-r.5 added as a runtime dependency (the DataCanvas engine), alongside `@cyanheads/mcp-ts-core` ^0.13.8.
